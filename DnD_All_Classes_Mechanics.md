# D&D 5e Classes & Subclasses Complete Mechanics

*Note: This document focuses entirely on strict mechanical rules (action economy, dice sizes, ranges, and level progression) to provide a complete, robust reference for your database.*

## Artificer
**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per artificer level
**Primary Ability:** Intelligence
**Saves:** Constitution, Intelligence
**Armor & Weapons:** Light, Medium, Shields, Simple weapons, Firearms
**Tools:** Thieves' tools, tinker's tools, one type of artisan's tools

### Core Features
*   **Magical Tinkering (Level 1):** Touch a Tiny nonmagical object to give it a magical property (emit light, play a message, emit odor, or show a picture). Max active objects = INT modifier.
*   **Spellcasting (Level 1):** INT based half-caster. Prepare spells daily (INT mod + half level). Can cast rituals. Tools required as spellcasting focus.
*   **Infuse Item (Level 2):** Learn specific Infusions to turn nonmagical objects into magic items. Known infusions scale from 4 to 12. Active infused items scale from 2 to 6.
*   **The Right Tool for the Job (Level 3):** Magically create any set of artisan's tools using tinker's tools (takes 1 hour).
*   **Tool Expertise (Level 6):** Proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check made that uses your proficiency with a tool.
*   **Flash of Genius (Level 7):** Reaction to add INT modifier to an ability check or saving throw made by you or a creature within 30 ft. Uses = INT mod per long rest.
*   **Magic Item Adept/Savant/Master (Level 10/14/18):** Attune to 4/5/6 magic items. Ignore class, race, spell, and level requirements for attuning.
*   **Spell-Storing Item (Level 11):** Store a 1st or 2nd-level artificer spell into a weapon or spellcasting focus. Can be used a number of times equal to 2 * INT modifier.
*   **Soul of Artifice (Level 20):** +1 bonus to all saving throws per magic item attuned. If reduced to 0 HP, can use a reaction to end an infusion to drop to 1 HP instead.

### Artificer Subclasses
**1. Alchemist**
*   **Tool Proficiency (Level 3):** Alchemist's supplies.
*   **Alchemist Spells (Level 3):** Healing Word, Ray of Sickness (3), Flaming Sphere, Melf's Acid Arrow (5), Gaseous Form, Mass Healing Word (9), Blight, Death Ward (13), Cloudkill, Raise Dead (17).
*   **Experimental Elixir (Level 3):** Create an elixir at the end of a long rest. Roll 1d6 for effect: Healing, Swiftness, Resilience, Boldness, Flight, Transformation. Can create more by expending spell slots.
*   **Alchemical Savant (Level 5):** Add INT mod to one damage or healing roll of spells cast using alchemist's supplies (acid, fire, necrotic, poison).
*   **Restorative Reagents (Level 9):** Creatures drinking your elixirs gain 2d6 + INT mod temporary HP. Cast Lesser Restoration INT mod times per long rest without a slot.
*   **Chemical Mastery (Level 15):** Resistance to acid and poison, immune to poisoned condition. Cast Greater Restoration and Heal once per long rest without a slot.

**2. Armorer**
*   **Tool Proficiency (Level 3):** Smith's tools.
*   **Armorer Spells (Level 3):** Magic Missile, Thunderwave (3), Mirror Image, Shatter (5), Hypnotic Pattern, Lightning Bolt (9), Fire Shield, Greater Invisibility (13), Pass Wall, Wall of Force (17).
*   **Arcane Armor (Level 3):** Turn armor into Arcane Armor. Cannot be removed against your will, expands to replace missing limbs. Negates STR requirement.
*   **Armor Model (Level 3):** Choose Guardian (Gauntlets deal 1d8 thunder and impose disadvantage on attacking others, gain temp HP) or Infiltrator (Lightning launcher deals 1d6 lightning, +5ft speed, advantage on Stealth).
*   **Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice instead of once.
*   **Armor Modifications (Level 9):** Armor counts as separate items for infusions (chest, boots, helmet, weapon). Two extra active infusions for armor only.
*   **Perfected Armor (Level 15):** Guardian (pull creatures 30ft to you), Infiltrator (target glows, disadvantage to hit you, next attack against it deals extra 1d6 lightning).

**3. Artillerist**
*   **Tool Proficiency (Level 3):** Woodcarver's tools.
*   **Artillerist Spells (Level 3):** Shield, Thunderwave (3), Scorching Ray, Shatter (5), Fireball, Wind Wall (9), Ice Storm, Wall of Fire (13), Cone of Cold, Wall of Force (17).
*   **Eldritch Cannon (Level 3):** Create a Small or Tiny cannon as an action. Choose: Flamethrower (15ft cone, 2d8 fire), Force Ballista (120ft, 2d8 force, push 5ft), Protector (10ft burst, 1d8+INT temp HP). Fires as bonus action.
*   **Arcane Firearm (Level 5):** Carve sigils into a wand, staff, or rod. Spells cast through it deal extra 1d8 damage.
*   **Explosive Cannon (Level 9):** Cannon damage increases by 1d8. Can detonate cannon as an action (20ft sphere, 3d8 force).
*   **Fortified Position (Level 15):** Cannon grants half cover to allies within 10ft. Can create two cannons simultaneously.

**4. Battle Smith**
*   **Tool Proficiency (Level 3):** Smith's tools.
*   **Battle Smith Spells (Level 3):** Heroism, Shield (3), Branding Smite, Warding Bond (5), Aura of Vitality, Conjure Barrage (9), Aura of Purity, Fire Shield (13), Banishing Smite, Mass Cure Wounds (17).
*   **Battle Ready (Level 3):** Martial weapon proficiency. Can use INT instead of STR/DEX for magic weapon attack/damage rolls.
*   **Steel Defender (Level 3):** Create a mechanical companion. Acts on your initiative after you. Dodge action by default. Bonus action to command it to Attack, Dash, Disengage, etc. Reaction to impose disadvantage on an attack against an ally.
*   **Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice instead of once.
*   **Arcane Jolt (Level 9):** When you or defender hit, deal extra 2d6 force damage or heal an ally for 2d6. Uses = INT mod per long rest.
*   **Improved Defender (Level 15):** Arcane Jolt increases to 4d6. Defender gets +2 AC and deals 1d4+INT force damage with its reaction deflection.

## Barbarian
**Hit Dice:** 1d12 per barbarian level
**Primary Ability:** Strength
**Saves:** Strength, Constitution
**Armor & Weapons:** Light, Medium, Shields, Simple, Martial

### Core Features
*   **Rage (Level 1):** Bonus action to enter. Advantage on STR checks/saves. Bonus to damage rolls (+2 to +4). Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing. Cannot cast spells. Lasts 1 minute.
*   **Unarmored Defense (Level 1):** AC = 10 + DEX mod + CON mod (can use shield).
*   **Reckless Attack (Level 2):** Advantage on STR melee attacks, but attacks against you have advantage until next turn.
*   **Danger Sense (Level 2):** Advantage on DEX saves against effects you can see.
*   **Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice.
*   **Fast Movement (Level 5):** +10 ft speed when not in heavy armor.
*   **Feral Instinct (Level 7):** Advantage on Initiative. Can ignore surprise if you rage immediately.
*   **Brutal Critical (Level 9/13/17):** Roll 1/2/3 extra weapon damage dice on a critical hit.
*   **Relentless Rage (Level 11):** If reduced to 0 HP while raging, make a CON save (DC 10, increases by 5 each use). On success, drop to 1 HP instead.
*   **Persistent Rage (Level 15):** Rage only ends early if you fall unconscious or choose to end it.
*   **Indomitable Might (Level 18):** If STR check is lower than STR score, use STR score instead.
*   **Primal Champion (Level 20):** STR and CON scores increase by 4. Maximum is now 24.

### Barbarian Subclasses
**1. Path of the Berserker**
*   **Frenzy (Level 3):** During rage, make a single melee weapon attack as a bonus action. Suffer 1 level of exhaustion when rage ends.
*   **Mindless Rage (Level 6):** Immune to charmed and frightened while raging.
*   **Intimidating Presence (Level 10):** Action to frighten a creature (WIS save).
*   **Retaliation (Level 14):** Reaction to make a melee attack against a creature that damages you within 5ft.

**2. Path of the Totem Warrior**
*   **Spirit Seeker (Level 3):** Cast Beast Sense and Speak with Animals as rituals.
*   **Totem Spirit (Level 3):** Choose Bear (resist all damage except psychic), Eagle (dash as bonus action, attacks of opportunity have disadvantage), Elk (+15ft speed), Tiger (+10ft jump), or Wolf (allies have advantage on melee attacks against enemies within 5ft of you).
*   **Aspect of the Beast (Level 6):** Bear (double carry capacity, advantage on push/pull), Eagle (see up to 1 mile perfectly), Elk (double travel pace), Tiger (two skill proficiencies), Wolf (track while traveling at fast pace).
*   **Spirit Walker (Level 10):** Cast Commune with Nature as a ritual.
*   **Totemic Attunement (Level 14):** Bear (impose disadvantage on enemies attacking allies), Eagle (gain fly speed while moving), Elk (bonus action move through enemies and knock prone), Tiger (bonus attack if you move 20ft before attack), Wolf (bonus action knock prone when you hit).

**3. Path of the Ancestral Guardian**
*   **Ancestral Protectors (Level 3):** First creature you hit while raging is distracted. Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you. If they hit an ally, ally resists the damage.
*   **Spirit Shield (Level 6):** Reaction to reduce damage taken by an ally within 30ft by 2d8 (scales to 3d8 at 10th, 4d8 at 14th).
*   **Consult the Spirits (Level 10):** Cast Clairvoyance or Augury once per short/long rest.
*   **Vengeful Ancestors (Level 14):** When using Spirit Shield, the attacker takes force damage equal to the damage prevented.

**4. Path of the Beast**
*   **Form of the Beast (Level 3):** Manifest natural weapons while raging. Bite (1d8, heals you proficiency bonus once per turn), Claws (1d6, one extra attack per turn), Tail (1d8, reaction to add 1d8 to AC against one attack).
*   **Bestial Soul (Level 6):** Natural weapons count as magical. Choose one adaptation per rest: Swim speed/breathe underwater, climb speed, or jumping requires no movement and adds Athletics check to distance.
*   **Infectious Fury (Level 10):** When you hit with natural weapons, force WIS save. On fail, target either takes 2d12 psychic damage or immediately attacks a creature of your choice. Uses = CON mod per long rest.
*   **Call the Hunt (Level 14):** When you rage, allies gain 1d6 bonus damage to one attack per turn. You gain 5 temp HP per ally that accepts this.

**5. Path of Wild Magic**
*   **Magic Awareness (Level 3):** Action to sense magic within 60ft (prof. bonus times per long rest).
*   **Wild Surge (Level 3):** Entering a rage triggers a random effect from the Wild Magic table (e.g., exploding flumphs, teleportation, light beams, returning weapons).
*   **Bolstering Magic (Level 6):** Touch a creature (uses = prof bonus). Either add 1d3 to their attack/ability checks for 10 mins, or restore a 1st-3rd level spell slot.
*   **Unstable Backlash (Level 10):** When you take damage or fail a save while raging, use reaction to immediately roll on the Wild Surge table, replacing the current effect.
*   **Controlled Surge (Level 14):** Roll twice on the Wild Surge table and choose which effect occurs.

## Bard
**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per bard level
**Primary Ability:** Charisma
**Saves:** Dexterity, Charisma
**Armor & Weapons:** Light, Simple, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords

### Core Features
*   **Spellcasting (Level 1):** CHA full-caster. Spells known system.
*   **Bardic Inspiration (Level 1):** Bonus action to grant a 1d6 die to an ally within 60ft. They can add it to an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. (Scales to 1d8 at 5th, 1d10 at 10th, 1d12 at 15th). Uses = CHA mod. Recharges on long rest (short rest at 5th level).
*   **Jack of All Trades (Level 2):** Add half proficiency bonus to any ability check that doesn't already use it (including Initiative).
*   **Song of Rest (Level 2):** Allies who spend hit dice during a short rest regain an extra 1d6 HP. (Scales to 1d8 at 9th, 1d10 at 13th, 1d12 at 17th).
*   **Expertise (Level 3/10):** Double proficiency bonus for 2 chosen skills (and 2 more at 10th).
*   **Font of Inspiration (Level 5):** Bardic Inspiration recharges on short or long rests.
*   **Countercharm (Level 6):** Action to play music; allies within 30ft have advantage on saves against frightened/charmed.
*   **Magical Secrets (Level 10/14/18):** Learn 2 spells from ANY class list. They count as bard spells.

### Bard Subclasses
**1. College of Lore**
*   **Bonus Proficiencies (Level 3):** 3 skill proficiencies of your choice.
*   **Cutting Words (Level 3):** Reaction to subtract Bardic Inspiration die from an enemy's attack roll, ability check, or damage roll within 60ft.
*   **Additional Magical Secrets (Level 6):** Learn 2 spells from any class list early.
*   **Peerless Skill (Level 14):** Expend Bardic Inspiration to add the die to your own ability check.

**2. College of Valor**
*   **Bonus Proficiencies (Level 3):** Medium armor, shields, martial weapons.
*   **Combat Inspiration (Level 3):** Allies can add the Bardic Inspiration die to weapon damage rolls, or use a reaction to add it to their AC against an attack.
*   **Extra Attack (Level 6):** Attack twice.
*   **Battle Magic (Level 14):** When you cast a bard spell as an action, make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

**3. College of Swords**
*   **Bonus Proficiencies (Level 3):** Medium armor, scimitars. Can use weapons as spellcasting foci.
*   **Fighting Style (Level 3):** Dueling or Two-Weapon Fighting.
*   **Blade Flourish (Level 3):** +10ft speed when attacking. Expend Bardic Inspiration to perform a flourish: Defensive (+AC), Slashing (damage another target), or Mobile (push target and move).
*   **Extra Attack (Level 6):** Attack twice.
*   **Master's Flourish (Level 14):** You can roll a d6 for a Blade Flourish instead of expending a Bardic Inspiration die.

**4. College of Eloquence**
*   **Silver Tongue (Level 3):** Treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower on Persuasion or Deception as a 10.
*   **Unsettling Words (Level 3):** Bonus action to expend a Bardic Inspiration die and subtract it from a creature's next saving throw.
*   **Unfailing Inspiration (Level 6):** If an ally's Bardic Inspiration roll fails to change an outcome, they keep the die.
*   **Universal Speech (Level 6):** Action to make a number of creatures equal to CHA mod magically understand you for 1 hour.
*   **Infectious Inspiration (Level 14):** When an ally succeeds using your Bardic Inspiration die, use reaction to grant another creature an Inspiration die without expending a use (Uses = CHA mod/long rest).

## Cleric
**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per cleric level
**Primary Ability:** Wisdom
**Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma
**Armor & Weapons:** Light, Medium, Shields, Simple

### Core Features
*   **Spellcasting (Level 1):** WIS full-caster. Prepared spells daily (WIS mod + level).
*   **Channel Divinity (Level 2):** Fuel magical effects. (1/rest at 2nd, 2/rest at 6th, 3/rest at 18th). Base effect: Turn Undead (WIS save or flee).
*   **Destroy Undead (Level 5/8/11/14/17):** Undead that fail Turn Undead save are instantly destroyed if CR is low enough.
*   **Divine Intervention (Level 10/20):** Action to request deity's aid. Roll percentile dice. If roll <= cleric level, deity intervenes. At level 20, succeeds automatically.

### Cleric Subclasses (Domains)
**1. Knowledge Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Command, Identify, Augury, Suggestion, Nondetection, Speak with Dead, Arcane Eye, Confusion, Legend Lore, Scrying.
*   **Blessings of Knowledge (Level 1):** 2 languages. Expertise in 2 skills (Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion).
*   **CD: Knowledge of the Ages (Level 2):** Gain proficiency with any one skill or tool for 10 mins.
*   **CD: Read Thoughts (Level 6):** Read surface thoughts of a creature (WIS save) for 1 min. Can cast Suggestion on them without a slot.
*   **Potent Spellcasting (Level 8):** Add WIS mod to cleric cantrip damage.
*   **Visions of the Past (Level 17):** Meditate to see past events of an object or area.

**2. Life Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Bless, Cure Wounds, Lesser Restoration, Spiritual Weapon, Beacon of Hope, Revivify, Death Ward, Guardian of Faith, Mass Cure Wounds, Raise Dead.
*   **Bonus Proficiency:** Heavy armor.
*   **Disciple of Life (Level 1):** Healing spells heal an additional 2 + spell level HP.
*   **CD: Preserve Life (Level 2):** Restore 5 * Cleric Level HP distributed among creatures within 30ft. Cannot heal past half HP max.
*   **Blessed Healer (Level 6):** When you cast a healing spell on another, heal yourself for 2 + spell level.
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Weapon attacks deal extra 1d8 radiant (2d8 at 14th).
*   **Supreme Healing (Level 17):** Healing spells automatically roll max dice.

**3. Light Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Burning Hands, Faerie Fire, Flaming Sphere, Scorching Ray, Daylight, Fireball, Wall of Fire, Guardian of Faith, Flame Strike, Scrying.
*   **Bonus Cantrip (Level 1):** Light.
*   **Warding Flare (Level 1):** Reaction to impose disadvantage on an attacker (Uses = WIS mod).
*   **CD: Radiance of the Dawn (Level 2):** 30ft burst. Dispels magical darkness. Enemies take 2d10 + cleric level radiant (CON save half).
*   **Improved Flare (Level 6):** Can use Warding Flare to protect allies.
*   **Potent Spellcasting (Level 8):** Add WIS mod to cantrip damage.
*   **Corona of Light (Level 17):** Action to emit 60ft aura. Enemies in it have disadvantage on saves against fire/radiant spells.

**4. Nature Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Animal Friendship, Speak with Animals, Barkskin, Spike Growth, Plant Growth, Wind Wall, Dominate Beast, Grasping Vine, Insect Plague, Tree Stride.
*   **Acolyte of Nature (Level 1):** Heavy armor proficiency. One druid cantrip. One skill (Animal Handling, Nature, Survival).
*   **CD: Charm Animals/Plants (Level 2):** Beasts/Plants within 30ft are charmed (WIS save).
*   **Dampen Elements (Level 6):** Reaction to grant yourself or ally within 30ft resistance to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder against one attack.
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 cold, fire, or lightning damage on weapon hit.
*   **Master of Nature (Level 17):** Command animals/plants charmed by your CD as a bonus action.

**5. Tempest Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Fog Cloud, Thunderwave, Gust of Wind, Shatter, Call Lightning, Sleet Storm, Control Water, Ice Storm, Destructive Wave, Insect Plague.
*   **Bonus Proficiencies:** Martial weapons, heavy armor.
*   **Wrath of the Storm (Level 1):** Reaction when hit, deal 2d8 lightning/thunder to attacker (DEX save half).
*   **CD: Destructive Wrath (Level 2):** When you deal lightning/thunder damage, use CD to roll max damage.
*   **Thunderous Strike (Level 6):** When you deal lightning damage to a Large or smaller creature, push it 10ft.
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 thunder damage on weapon hit.
*   **Stormborn (Level 17):** Gain flying speed outdoors.

**6. Trickery Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Charm Person, Disguise Self, Mirror Image, Pass without Trace, Blink, Dispel Magic, Dimension Door, Polymorph, Dominate Person, Modify Memory.
*   **Blessing of the Trickster (Level 1):** Touch ally to give them advantage on Stealth.
*   **CD: Invoke Duplicity (Level 2):** Create a perfect illusion of yourself. Move it as bonus action. Cast spells through it. Grants advantage to your attacks if both adjacent to enemy.
*   **CD: Cloak of Shadows (Level 6):** Use CD to become invisible until end of next turn or attack/cast.
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 poison damage on weapon hit.
*   **Improved Duplicity (Level 17):** Create up to 4 duplicates.

**7. War Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Divine Favor, Shield of Faith, Magic Weapon, Spiritual Weapon, Crusader's Mantle, Spirit Guardians, Freedom of Movement, Stoneskin, Flame Strike, Hold Monster.
*   **Bonus Proficiencies:** Martial weapons, heavy armor.
*   **War Priest (Level 1):** When you use Attack action, make a weapon attack as bonus action (WIS mod times/day).
*   **CD: Guided Strike (Level 2):** +10 to an attack roll.
*   **CD: War God's Blessing (Level 6):** Grant +10 attack roll to an ally within 30ft (Reaction).
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 weapon damage of same type.
*   **Avatar of Battle (Level 17):** Resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, slashing.

**8. Forge Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Identify, Searing Smite, Heat Metal, Magic Weapon, Elemental Weapon, Protection from Energy, Fabricate, Wall of Fire, Animate Objects, Creation.
*   **Bonus Proficiencies:** Heavy armor, smith's tools.
*   **Blessing of the Forge (Level 1):** Touch armor/weapon at long rest end. It becomes +1.
*   **CD: Artisan's Blessing (Level 2):** 1-hour ritual to create nonmagical item < 100gp. Consume equivalent metal value.
*   **Soul of the Forge (Level 6):** Resistance to fire. +1 AC while in heavy armor.
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 fire damage on weapon hit.
*   **Saint of Forge and Fire (Level 17):** Immunity to fire. Resistance to nonmagical weapon damage.

**9. Grave Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Bane, False Life, Gentle Repose, Ray of Enfeeblement, Revivify, Vampiric Touch, Blight, Death Ward, Antilife Shell, Raise Dead.
*   **Circle of Mortality (Level 1):** Healing spells on 0 HP targets roll max dice. Gain Spare the Dying (30ft range, bonus action).
*   **Eyes of the Grave (Level 1):** Detect undead within 60ft (WIS mod times/day).
*   **CD: Path to the Grave (Level 2):** Curse a creature. The next time you/ally hit it, it has vulnerability to all of that attack's damage.
*   **Sentinel at Death's Door (Level 6):** Reaction to turn an enemy's critical hit into a normal hit (WIS mod times/day).
*   **Potent Spellcasting (Level 8):** Add WIS mod to cantrip damage.
*   **Keeper of Souls (Level 17):** When enemy dies within 30ft, you or ally regain HP equal to its hit dice.

**10. Order Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Command, Heroism, Hold Person, Zone of Truth, Mass Healing Word, Slow, Compulsion, Locate Creature, Commune, Dominate Person.
*   **Bonus Proficiencies:** Heavy armor, Intimidation or Persuasion.
*   **Voice of Authority (Level 1):** When you cast a 1st-level+ spell on an ally, they can use reaction to make one weapon attack.
*   **CD: Order's Demand (Level 2):** Charm enemies within 30ft (WIS save). They drop what they are holding.
*   **Embodiment of the Law (Level 6):** Cast enchantment spells as a bonus action (WIS mod times/day).
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 psychic damage on weapon hit.
*   **Order's Wrath (Level 17):** When you deal Divine Strike damage, curse target. Next ally attack against it deals extra 2d8 psychic.

**11. Twilight Domain**
*   **Domain Spells:** Faerie Fire, Sleep, Moonbeam, See Invisibility, Aura of Vitality, Leomund's Tiny Hut, Aura of Life, Greater Invisibility, Circle of Power, Mislead.
*   **Bonus Proficiencies:** Martial weapons, heavy armor.
*   **Eyes of Night (Level 1):** 300ft darkvision. Can share it with allies for 1 hr.
*   **Vigilant Blessing (Level 1):** Grant advantage on next Initiative roll to a creature.
*   **CD: Twilight Sanctuary (Level 2):** 30ft aura. At end of ally's turn in aura, give them 1d6+Cleric level temp HP OR end a charm/frightened condition.
*   **Steps of Night (Level 6):** Gain fly speed in dim light/darkness (bonus action).
*   **Divine Strike (Level 8):** Extra 1d8 radiant damage on weapon hit.
*   **Twilight Shroud (Level 17):** Twilight Sanctuary aura grants half cover.

## Druid
**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per druid level
**Primary Ability:** Wisdom
**Saves:** Intelligence, Wisdom
**Armor & Weapons:** Light, Medium, Shields (non-metal), Clubs, daggers, darts, javelins, maces, quarterstaffs, scimitars, sickles, slings, spears.

### Core Features
*   **Druidic (Level 1):** Secret language.
*   **Spellcasting (Level 1):** WIS full-caster. Prepared spells daily.
*   **Wild Shape (Level 2):** Action to magically assume the shape of a beast you have seen. 2/short rest. HP pools are separate. CR max: 1/4 (Level 2), 1/2 (Level 4), 1 (Level 8).
*   **Timeless Body (Level 18):** Age 1 year for every 10 passed.
*   **Beast Spells (Level 18):** Cast verbal/somatic spells in beast form.
*   **Archdruid (Level 20):** Unlimited Wild Shape. Ignore V/S and non-costly M components.

### Druid Subclasses (Circles)
**1. Circle of the Land**
*   **Bonus Cantrip (Level 2):** One extra druid cantrip.
*   **Natural Recovery (Level 2):** Regain spell slots equal to half druid level during short rest (once per day).
*   **Circle Spells (Level 3/5/7/9):** Always prepared spells based on chosen biome (Arctic, Coast, Desert, Forest, Grassland, Mountain, Swamp, Underdark).
*   **Land's Stride (Level 6):** Move through nonmagical difficult terrain without penalty. Advantage on saves vs magical plants.
*   **Nature's Ward (Level 10):** Immune to poison and disease. Cannot be charmed/frightened by fey/elementals.
*   **Nature's Sanctuary (Level 14):** Beasts/Plants must make WIS save to attack you.

**2. Circle of the Moon**
*   **Combat Wild Shape (Level 2):** Wild Shape as a bonus action. Can expend spell slots to heal while shifted (1d8 per level).
*   **Circle Forms (Level 2):** Transform into CR 1 beasts immediately. Scales to Druid Level / 3.
*   **Primal Strike (Level 6):** Beast attacks count as magical.
*   **Elemental Wild Shape (Level 10):** Expend 2 uses of Wild Shape to turn into an Air, Earth, Fire, or Water Elemental.
*   **Thousand Forms (Level 14):** Cast Alter Self at will.

**3. Circle of the Shepherd**
*   **Speech of the Woods (Level 2):** Speak with animals constantly. Learn Sylvan.
*   **Spirit Totem (Level 2):** Bonus action to summon an aura (30ft) for 1 minute. Bear (temp HP), Hawk (advantage on perception, reaction to give advantage to ally attack), Unicorn (advantage to detect enemies, healing spells heal aura allies).
*   **Mighty Summoner (Level 6):** Summoned beasts/fey have +2 HP per hit die and their attacks are magical.
*   **Guardian Spirit (Level 10):** Summoned beasts/fey heal 1/2 druid level each turn they end in your Spirit Totem aura.
*   **Faithful Summons (Level 14):** If you drop to 0 HP, automatically summon 4 CR 2 beasts to defend you.

**4. Circle of the Spores**
*   **Halo of Spores (Level 2):** Reaction when enemy moves within 10ft: deal 1d4 necrotic (CON save). Scales to 1d6 (6th), 1d8 (10th), 1d10 (14th).
*   **Symbiotic Entity (Level 2):** Expend Wild Shape to gain 4 temp HP per druid level. Double Halo of Spores damage. Melee weapon attacks deal extra 1d6 necrotic. Lasts 10 mins or until temp HP gone.
*   **Fungal Infestation (Level 6):** Reaction when humanoid/beast dies within 10ft to raise it as a zombie with 1 HP.
*   **Spreading Spores (Level 10):** Bonus action to throw spores up to 30ft (10ft cube). Deals Halo damage.
*   **Fungal Body (Level 14):** Immune to blind, deaf, frighten, poison. Critical hits against you count as normal hits if you aren't incapacitated.

**5. Circle of Stars**
*   **Star Map (Level 2):** Guidance cantrip. Free Guiding Bolt castings equal to prof bonus.
*   **Starry Form (Level 2):** Expend Wild Shape to gain starry form. Shed light. Choose constellation: Archer (bonus action luminous arrow attack 1d8+WIS), Chalice (extra 1d8+WIS healing when casting heal spells), Dragon (treat INT/WIS checks and concentration saves < 10 as 10).
*   **Cosmic Omen (Level 6):** Roll d6 at long rest. Even (Weal): Reaction to add 1d6 to a creature's roll. Odd (Woe): Reaction to subtract 1d6.

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity & Wisdom
**Saves:** Strength, Dexterity

*   **Physician's Touch (Level 6):** Hand of Healing cures disease/blind/deaf/paralyzed/poisoned. Hand of Harm inflicts poisoned condition.
*   **Flurry of Healing and Harm (Level 11):** When using Flurry of Blows, you can use Hand of Healing AND Hand of Harm once each without spending their separate Ki costs.

*   **Tentacle of the Deeps (Level 1):** Bonus action summon tentacle (60ft). Make spell attack, deals 1d8 cold and reduces target speed by 10ft.
*   **Gift of the Sea (Level 1):** 40ft swim speed. Breathe underwater.
*   **Oceanic Soul (Level 6):** Resist cold damage. Communicate with underwater creatures.
*   **Guardian Coil (Level 6):** Tentacle can use reaction to reduce incoming damage to ally by 1d8.
*   **Grasping Tentacles (Level 10):** Cast Evard's Black Tentacles. Temp HP equal to warlock level when cast.
*   **Fathomless Plunge (Level 14):** Action to teleport self and up to 5 willing creatures via body of water to another known body of water (1 mile).

## Wizard
**Hit Dice:** 1d6 per wizard level
**Primary Ability:** Intelligence
**Saves:** Intelligence, Wisdom
**Armor & Weapons:** None, daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows.

### Core Features
*   **Spellcasting (Level 1):** INT full-caster. Spellbook system. Can cast unprepared rituals if in book.
*   **Arcane Recovery (Level 1):** Once per day on short rest, recover spell slots equal to half wizard level.
*   **Spell Mastery (Level 18):** Choose one 1st-level and one 2nd-level spell to cast at-will.
*   **Signature Spells (Level 20):** Choose two 3rd-level spells, cast each once per rest for free.

### Wizard Subclasses (Arcane Traditions)
**1. Abjuration**
*   **Arcane Ward (Level 2):** Casting an abjuration spell creates a ward (HP = 2*Level + INT). Takes damage instead of you.
*   **Projected Ward (Level 6):** Reaction to apply ward to an ally taking damage.
*   **Improved Abjuration (Level 10):** Add prof bonus to ability checks for Counterspell / Dispel Magic.
*   **Spell Resistance (Level 14):** Advantage on spell saves. Resistance to spell damage.

**2. Conjuration**
*   **Minor Conjuration (Level 2):** Action to conjure inanimate, nonmagical object in hand (lasts 1 hr).
*   **Benign Transposition (Level 6):** Action teleport 30ft or swap places with willing ally. Recharges on casting conjuration spell.
*   **Focused Conjuration (Level 10):** Cannot lose concentration on conjuration spells from taking damage.
*   **Durable Summons (Level 14):** Summoned creatures gain 30 temp HP.

**3. Divination**
*   **Portent (Level 2):** Roll 2d20s at long rest end. Replace any attack/save/check with these rolls. (3d20s at 14th).
*   **Expert Divination (Level 6):** Casting a divination spell 2nd level+ restores a spell slot of a lower level.
*   **The Third Eye (Level 10):** Action to gain darkvision, ethereal sight, read all languages, or see invisibility.

**4. Enchantment**
*   **Hypnotic Gaze (Level 2):** Action to charm adjacent creature, incapacitating it (WIS save). Extend duration with action.
*   **Instinctive Charm (Level 6):** Reaction to force an attacker to target someone else (WIS save).
*   **Split Enchantment (Level 10):** Single-target enchantment spells can target two creatures.
*   **Alter Memories (Level 14):** Charmed creatures don't know they were charmed. Can erase hours of memory.

**5. Evocation**
*   **Sculpt Spells (Level 2):** Choose 1+spell level targets to automatically pass saves and take 0 damage from your evocation spells.
*   **Potent Cantrip (Level 6):** Enemies take half damage on cantrip saves instead of zero.
*   **Empowered Evocation (Level 10):** Add INT mod to evocation spell damage rolls.
*   **Overchannel (Level 14):** Maximize damage of 1st-5th level spells. Take necrotic damage if used multiple times per rest.

**6. Illusion**
*   **Improved Minor Illusion (Level 2):** Learn cantrip. Can create sound and image simultaneously.
*   **Malleable Illusions (Level 6):** Action to change parameters of an active illusion spell.
*   **Illusory Self (Level 10):** Reaction to make an attack automatically miss by imposing a duplicate.
*   **Illusory Reality (Level 14):** Make one inanimate object part of an illusion real for 1 minute.

**7. Necromancy**
*   **Grim Harvest (Level 2):** Killing enemy with a spell heals you (2 * spell level HP, 3x if necromancy).
*   **Undead Thralls (Level 6):** Animate Dead targets +1 corpse. Summons get +Max HP (wizard level) and add prof bonus to damage.
*   **Inured to Undeath (Level 10):** Resistant to necrotic damage. Max HP cannot be reduced.
*   **Command Undead (Level 14):** Action to force an undead to make CHA save. On fail, it serves you permanently.

**8. Transmutation**
*   **Minor Alchemy (Level 2):** Wood/stone/iron/copper/silver transmutations (1 hour process).
*   **Transmuter's Stone (Level 6):** Create a stone granting darkvision, +10 speed, CON save prof, or element resist.
*   **Shapechanger (Level 10):** Cast Polymorph on self (CR 1 or lower) without a slot.
*   **Master Transmuter (Level 14):** Destroy stone to: Cast Panacea (Heal + conditions), Restore life (Raise Dead), Restore Youth, or Transform huge objects.

**9. Bladesinging**
*   **Training in War and Song (Level 2):** Light armor, 1 melee weapon prof, Performance prof.
*   **Bladesong (Level 2):** Bonus action (Prof. uses/day). Add INT to AC, +10ft speed, advantage on Acrobatics, add INT to concentration saves.
*   **Extra Attack (Level 6):** Attack twice. Can replace one attack with a Cantrip.
*   **Song of Defense (Level 10):** Reaction, expend spell slot to reduce damage by 5 * slot level.
*   **Song of Victory (Level 14):** Add INT mod to melee weapon damage while Bladesinging.

## Blood Hunter (2020 Edition)
**Hit Dice:** 1d10 per level
**Primary Ability:** Dexterity/Strength & Intelligence
**Saves:** Dexterity, Intelligence

### Core Features
*   **Hunter's Bane (Level 1):** Advantage on tracking fey, fiends, undead.
*   **Blood Maledict (Level 1):** Learn Blood Curses. Amplify by taking damage = Hemocraft die (1d4 scales to 1d10).
*   **Crimson Rite (Level 2):** Infuse weapon with elemental energy. Take necrotic damage (reduces max HP). Attacks deal extra Hemocraft die damage.
*   **Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice.
*   **Brand of Castigation (Level 6):** Brand an enemy hit by Crimson Rite. Deal INT mod psychic damage when they attack you.

### Blood Hunter Subclasses
**1. Order of the Ghostslayer**
*   **Rite of the Dawn (Level 3):** Radiant rite damage. Double damage to undead. Doesn't reduce max HP when targeting undead.
*   **Curse Specialist (Level 3):** Curses affect bloodless creatures.
*   **Ethereal Step (Level 7):** Walk through creatures/objects.
*   **Brand of Sundering (Level 11):** Brand prevents moving through objects/invisibility.
*   **Blood Curse of the Exorcist (Level 15):** End charm/frighten/possession.

**2. Order of the Lycan**
*   **Hybrid Transformation (Level 3):** Bonus action transform. Feral Might (+damage, adv STR), Resilient Hide (+AC, resist nonmagical B/P/S), Predatory Strikes (1d6 unarmed).
*   **Bloodlust (Level 3):** If < 50% HP, WIS save or attack nearest creature.
*   **Advanced Transformation (Level 11):** Regen HP if < 50% HP.
*   **Hybrid Terror Form (Level 18):** Amplify curses in hybrid form without taking damage.

**3. Order of the Mutant**
*   **Mutagencraft (Level 3):** Craft mutagens. Give buffs but inflict drawbacks (e.g., +STR but disadvantage DEX saves).
*   **Strange Metabolism (Level 7):** Ignore mutagen side effect for 1 minute.
*   **Brand of Axiom (Level 11):** Force shapeshifters into true form.

**4. Order of the Profane Soul**
*   **Pact Magic (Level 3):** Warlock spellcasting (max 4th level slots).
*   **Patron Features (Level 3):** Rite gains effects based on chosen Warlock patron.
*   **Mystic Frenzy (Level 7):** Cantrip as action -> weapon attack as bonus action.
*   **Brand of the Scar (Level 11):** Branded creature takes extra hemocraft die from spells.
