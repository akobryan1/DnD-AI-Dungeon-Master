# Al'mundi Worldbuilding Overview

## 1. General Overview

Over the centuries, the many species that inhabit Al'mundi have traveled and mingled in many different ways as neighbors tend to do. However, each species still has a preferred climate to inhabit (though there are always outliers).

### Species & Preferred Climates
* **Elves & High Elves:** Prefer to inhabit forests, in which they feel more connected to nature and where they learned the magic of the world.
* **Dwarves & Hill Dwarves:** Dwarves prefer to inhabit mountains, while their cousins the hill dwarves prefer the neighboring foothills.
* **Halflings & Lightfoot Halflings:** Halflings prefer to live in lush, temperate regions, while their cousins the Lightfoot halflings tend to be found in marshes and swamps, where they learned to step carefully.
* **Humans:** Comfortable in every region of the continent, and as such, are the most widespread.
* **Gnomes & Rock Gnomes:** Gnomes can be found anywhere, but are particularly in sprawling savannas and prairies. Their cousins, rock gnomes, prefer mountainous regions and often share living areas with dwarves.
* **Dragonborn:** Few as they are, prefer the hot, arid regions of Al'mundi, where they are generally left alone.
* **Tieflings:** Found scattered about the continent, but rumors suggest that they can be found more prominently near volcanoes, or perhaps in the farthest regions of the continent where daylight is scarce.
* **Half-Elves & Half-Orcs:** Can be found basically anywhere, and for a long time, struggled to fit in within their respective cultures—on either, or both, sides of their parentage.

## 2. Prominent Regions

### Forest of Allanar
The northeastern part of the continent was once the elven Kingdom of Allanar. Although the kingdom fell to the tides of time long ago, the region is still often called Allanar, or the Forest of Allanar. This is because almost 95% of the region is a single, sprawling, 200-mile, arboreal forest. 

Nestled in the heart of Allanar Forest is the great city of Æressari—once the seat of power for the Kingdom of Allanar, and now the oldest city in the continent. Still to this day, the region is mostly populated by elves, with a handful of human, gnomish, and halfling settlements scattered about in clearings and rivers throughout the massive forest. The widest river in Allanar is the Ghivanni River, which is enchanted to always be safe to drink and is a quarter of a mile wide at its widest point.

### Khigvorda Mountains
This towering mountain range is called the Iron Ridge Mountains by humans and many other species, as they argue that Khigvorda is too hard to pronounce. These mountains contain the three tallest peaks in the world, as well as the oldest active mine (Khikaghan Mine) in the continent. 

Made almost entirely of various types of granite, these mountains are notoriously hard to mine, dig, and construct upon. There are only a handful of mines within the mountains, and even the Dwarves and Stone Gnomes have struggled to build into the mountains without the aid of strong sorcery. It is a harsh, desolate place where maps are rarely accurate. The great Dwarven city of Veladhur sits upon one of the smaller mountains on the edge of the Khigvorda Mountains—one of the few mountains that wasn't dense granite.

### Ada-vianelle - The Fading Swamp
Far south, there is a thick swamp that only the most adventurous heroes—and native Lightfoot halflings as well as the occasional elf—dare to tread. Many who enter the swamp (which spreads across the land for almost fifty miles) never return. Monsters lurk in its brackish water, and some rumors suggest that the trees actually move and eat unlucky travelers. 

Those that have survived the journey say that the swamp seems to fade in and out of existence, taking wanderers with it—which is where it got its nickname. There are two known settlements within the Fading Swamp, Istegan and Varapor. Both are townships of Lightfoot halflings, though some suggest that there are swamp folk that live so far in the marsh that they have never encountered an outsider before.

### Starview Plains
Named after the sprawling sky of stars visible at night upon the plains, the plains once had another name in elvish that has been long forgotten. Humans, Gnomes, Halflings, Tieflings, Half-Elves, and Half-Orcs inhabit small to medium-sized villages scattered throughout the plains. 

These villages are mostly composed of one or two races, although some may have more diversity. They tend to keep to themselves but do like to trade amongst the nearby villages. Many of these are farming villages that may focus on crops, livestock, and fishing. Most people here enjoy living a more simple and peaceful life in opposition to the bustling cities.

### The Merchant's Pass
Leskyir is a bustling city located in the center of the continent and is the hub for trade between different regions of the world. It is known for its strategic location and the vast caravans that pass through it. The environment surrounding the city is mostly barren, with sand and sandstone as the dominant features. 

The buildings in the city are also made predominantly of sandstone, with most of the houses set up as tents made from blankets and wood. Despite the landscape around Leskyir being quite barren and plain, Leskyir is known for being full of colorful banners and paintings which intend to attract merchants to goods as they pass through the city. The city attracts merchants, traders, and adventurers from all over the world, and is always abuzz with activity. The desert surrounding Leskyir is home to a number of small settlements and villages, each with their own unique character and culture. These settlements are often centers for local trade and commerce, and many are populated by nomadic tribes and travelers.

## 3. Prominent Cities

### City 1: Æressari

#### General Overview
The city of Æressari is considered to be one of the Great Wonders of the continent and is the oldest city in the continent. It sits in the northeastern portion of Al'mundi, with mountains protecting its northern border, and is constructed almost entirely from living wood and warm marble melded from the earth. For centuries, Æressari was the capital of the Elven Kingdom, and even after the Kingdom fell, it was enchanted so that only Elves, High Elves, and Half-Elves could find the city. 

These days, outsiders are welcome, but the population is still 65% Elvish. Magic flows through almost every inch of the ancient city, and it is considered a crime punishable by death to intentionally cut, chop, demolish, or destroy any of the living buildings within the city. As such, Æressari has expanded little over the years—as the ancient spells used to craft the city from nature itself were lost long ago. So, the city hasn't grown nor shrunk much. 

The city has been considered the center of all magical knowledge and power for the majority of history. Powerful mages flock there, and many remain and live within the city—finding the ancient magic and living city soothing.

#### History
The city was sung into existence by the High Elves in Ages past. It is a wondrous beauty of architecture and nature. Every building is seamlessly crafted from the earth or trees themselves and remains alive. Flowers, native fauna, and harmless insects flitter about everywhere. Life is celebrated in the city, and it is considered to be the cultural birthplace of magic. 

Æressari has survived the rise and fall of several ages, kingdoms, and dynasties. Its ancient magic has prevented its destruction, though it has also limited its growth. After the fall of the Kingdom of Allanar, the governance of the city was handed over to a Council of Elders, who still rule to this day. Æressari has always been the voice of reason and peace in global politics, though they understand that not everyone will listen. They are firmly neutral in all external conflicts since the fall of Allanar and remain a refuge for all except rapists and child murderers. 

Although Æressari seems like a paradise to outsiders, the city has become increasingly classist over the last couple of centuries, and although nature is still valued, Æressari citizens have begun to take their magnificent home for granted.

#### Appearance and Layout
The city of Æressari stretches in a circle for nearly five miles and is a breathing, sprawling metropolis of trees dotted with marble towers. Its highest tree-tower reaches a hundred stories tall. When entering the city through one of the many forest roads, one of the first things noticeable about Æressari is the towering oak with red bark and white leaves that sits in the center of the city. This magnificent oak is the Æressari University.

The city is structurally layered into distinct social and architectural zones:
* **The Branches (or Flower Homes):** Surrounding the massive center oak are smaller trees, enchanted to always bloom, where the wealthy, powerful, and aristocrats live. They have homes spun by twisting branches and adorned with ever-blooming flowers. Among these towering, flowering trees are twisting towers of molded marble, sung from the earth without a single seam. These serve as government buildings, wizard laboratories, and mage towers, known simply as **The Towers**.
* **The Hearth:** Workshops, marketplaces, and bookstores are scattered about nooks in hollowed tree trunks and twisted lower branches. This is where most of the middle-class live.
* **The Burrows:** The rest of the population dwells in small marble buildings scattered amongst the tree roots, or small homes in the bases of the trees. This is where the lower class and destitute live. The Burrows are just as beautiful as the Branches, but many residents have forgotten this, distancing those living on the lower levels. Æressari has the biggest homeless/slum population out of the Three Great Cities.

#### Significant Locations
* **Æressari University:** Once the Palace of the Allanar Kingdom, it hosts hundreds of classrooms within its branches and shelters thousands of magic users of all kinds. It studies all branches of magic (although some are only theoretical) and holds the belief that Magic is the best craft of them all. Æressari University is a bitter rival of Emberden College.
* **City Tower:** The city hall of Æressari sits directly north of the University in the tallest marble tower. This is where the High Council gathers, and where some of the Council members live.
* **Orchid's Inn:** The most exclusive Inn in the city. There is a fee in gold or a magical feat required at the door just to gain entry. It is run by a mysterious mage simply known as Orchid, a title believed to be inherited because they have been around for so long.
* **Nature's Garden:** A large marketplace nestled to the south of the University that spans several stories.
* **Firefly's Dream:** An inn and tavern located in the Burrows. It is run by an elderly elf named Sylaquin (who refuses to give a last name). If someone cannot pay for a room or food, Sylaquin trades services or info for shelter and food.
* **Moonlit Forge:** The best blacksmith shop in Æressari, run by Ole Luriant. It is owned by a dwarf named Ghan, but he is never in the city, so it is basically Ole's business.
* **Treasure Trove:** A merchant shop in Nature's Garden that sells odds, ends, and everything in between. It is owned by a halfling named Lila Rainview.

#### Prominent People
* **The High Council:** There are seven seats upon the high council, and each seat is inherited or chosen internally, not elected. The current members are:
    * *Lazani Bloom* (elderly High Elf)
    * *Zorwyn Ridanna* (Previous Head Mage, also an elf)
    * *Eric Fenro* (powerful sorcerer, half-elf)
    * *Miraka Starglen* (heiress, High Elf)
    * *Nerine Oaskly* (powerful wizard, halfling)
    * *Ollenia Feshard* (powerful druid, elf)
    * *Sephon Ambervan* (wealthy trade lord, elf)
* **Orchid:** A mysterious mage of indeterminable gender who owns and operates Orchid's Inn. No one knows anything about them, except that Orchid must be an inherited title because they have been around forever.
* **Head Mage Byran Emoxia:** Headmistress of the University, Byran is a powerful mages and subtle politician. She has blackmail material on several council members and often dismisses anyone who doesn't practice magic as unimportant. She loves rare magical artifacts.
* **Sylaquin:** Owner of Firefly's Dream, this elderly elf makes sure all of the children in the city have a warm hearth if they need one. She is also an info broker who trades favors. There are rumors that she was once an Elf of high society who left it all behind.
* **Ole Luriant:** An elven blacksmith who runs the Moonlit Forge. He is the first High Elf to go to the Craftsman College in Veladhur.
* **Lila Rainview:** A young halfling woman who owns the Treasure Trove. She is courting a young shoemaker gnome named Lop and is madly in love with him, much to the disappointment of her parents.

#### Nearby Townships and Villages
* **Teafield:** A halfling village located in the largest clearing (five miles wide) in Allanar, about twenty miles from Æressari. They are known for their exquisite tea exports. Some humans, elves, and half-elves have settled there to learn the tea trade.
* **Stormwood:** A halfling and elven founded town at the eastern edge of Allanar Forest, where storms sweep in off of the Eastern Sea almost constantly.
* **Esalym:** An elven town on the Eastern Coast that features both forest and beach. It is a popular tourist destination for its area's beauty and hosts the best health retreat/hospital on the continent.
* **Oakwood:** The only human settlement within Allanar. The town has a strict treaty with the local elves about how much lumber they are allowed to fell and process. The different perspectives on forests and lumberjacks between the two races cause tension. It is only due to the halfling mayor that a peace treaty was established at all.
* **Strauvani:** A township originally founded by gnomes south of Æressari. A common stop along many trade routes, the town relies heavily on its many inns for business.
* **Cerallya:** Deep in the heart of Allanar, this High Elven town is one of the few ancient elven settlements left in Allanar. No one has been able to successfully find the town if they have not been there before, and it is believed to be enchanted against non-elves.
* **Andizyre:** The only known Tiefling town in the world, settled just north of the northern Allanar border in the northern wilds of the continent. The township gets few visitors and is often plagued by monsters—but that has never been a problem for its residents. They sell monster parts, pelts, and scales for revenue, but otherwise ignore the rest of the continent.

### City 2: Veladhur

#### General Overview
Veladhur is an ancient dwarven city, but it is not their first nor their oldest. It is the oldest one still standing, however, as a great earthquake destroyed the First City. Veladhur is carved from a mountain peak on one of the smallest mountains (its previous name long forgotten) on the edge of the Khigvorda Mountains. It is an ancient seat of trade for the entire area, where the various ores and gems of the nearby Khikaghan Mine are transformed into tools, jewelry, ingots, etc.

The Khigvorda Mountains are fairly desolate, so life in Veladhur is difficult and frugal. Thus, while many people travel to the grand city, its population is the smallest of the Three Cities and is mostly inhabited by dwarves, gnomes, and halflings. The city is incredibly wealthy and hasn't expanded much over the years. In fact, the city currently has half the population it did two centuries previously, leaving many empty buildings. However, despite its small population, Veladhur has the most capable City Guard on the continent, though their focus is more on repelling monsters from the mountain range than catching every criminal in the city.

#### History
Veladhur was first founded by the miners of Khikaghan, as the mountain was easier to dig into than the mine and houses went up quickly. It rapidly became a trade and artisan hub thanks to the mine, eventually expanding to consume the entire mountain peak. After the Dwarves lost their First City, much of the dwarven culture, government, and craftsmen moved to Veladhur and carved into the mountain until nothing of the peak remained—only the city.

Designed and enchanted to survive every test of time, rumors suggest that Veladhur is indestructible. It has withstood sieges and dragon attacks with minimal effort. The metalsmiths and gem workers of Veladhur are the best in the world, and many adventurers travel there to commission armor and weapons. The inhabitants joke that Veladhur will stand until the end of time—or until the Khikaghan Mine runs dry, which would be the end of all things.

#### Appearance and Layout
Veladhur is often mistaken to be built on top of a flat mountain peak, but it is actually carved *from* the mountain peak until the peak itself was completely replaced by the city, sitting upon the shorter mountain like a crown. Every building is carved from the dark stone and softer granite of the mountain. Because every building is constructed from the same material, the city is heavily decorated with carvings, a variety of architectural styles, and vibrant paint, making it much brighter and more colorful than travelers expect.

Unlike other cities, Veladhur isn't obviously sectioned off into distinct wealth districts. Businesses and homes are spread evenly throughout the city, and Veladhur has the smallest poor district of the Three Cities because unemployment is practically nonexistent. There is always work to be done in the Mines or the Guard, and the city is the only one on the continent that willingly pays disability to those grievously injured or disabled in the line of work (regardless of profession).

The tallest, central part of the city is called **The Heart**, which is where the best craftsmen work and live. Otherwise, the city is evenly distributed. There are only two large "estates" in the city: one belonging to the Craftsmen College and the other to the Blacksmith Guild. Even Veladhur's city hall is a quarter of the size of the College. Due to the towering mountains behind the city, the sun isn't visible until almost noon and quickly vanishes behind the foothills in the west. Instead, the city is lit with enchanted crystalline lanterns that give off a bright, clear luminescence highly praised by artisans.

#### Significant Locations
* **Craftsmen College:** The largest building in the city, featuring thick wards and silver outer walls. The College trains all who approach its gates and offers work-study scholarships (covering food and materials) for those who cannot afford tuition. They believe everyone who wishes to learn a craft should have the opportunity, offering almost every trade and craft in the world, but fewer "classical" academics.
* **Blacksmith Guild:** The second largest building in town, its entire exterior is carved with a massive mosaic of the Dwarves' racial history. They allow all species into their halls but tend to favor dwarven members. Membership acts as a quality assurance license, and the Guild helps new artisans move or start shops in other cities for a percentage fee.
* **City Hall:** Painted a pale blue to match the sky, it is decorated with a shifting mosaic/map of Veladhur that updates automatically as the city changes. It holds almost all of the important offices in the city as well as several meeting rooms.
* **The Empty Mine Hostel:** The oldest hostel and tavern in the city, actually predating most of Veladhur. It is a simple, two-story building with no paint, featuring subtle carvings of working miners into its door frames and window ledges. It is currently owned by a gnomish woman named Abelina Sharpstone.
* **Stonebed Inn:** Significantly larger than the Empty Mine, it is the fifth largest building in Veladhur. Because the city is always swelling with tourists, travelers, adventurers, and learning craftsmen, this copper-colored building features over a hundred rooms and doubles as a tavern. It is decorated with artistic mountains carved into its walls and clouds upon its roof. It is owned and operated by Lezne and Quin Dargildan, a dwarven couple.
* **Copper Spout Tavern:** The only tavern in town that is not also an inn or hostel. It is a recently refurbished building that was once an alchemist's workshop, getting its name from its half-transmuted copper walls. It is owned and run by Hunter Sterkan, a young rock gnome.
* **Crow's Nest:** The largest merchant shop in the city, said to have anything and everything for sale if you can find it. The owner, a human named Durwin Thap, is a terrible hoarder and often prefers trades over coin payments.
* **Glitter 'n Gold:** The most famous jeweler and gem worker shop in the city, frequently taking commissions from wealthy patrons across the world. They often hire adventurers to guard their deliveries. It is owned by the dwarven Ortrevuni brothers, and security is overseen by Veccan Thesierra.
* **Ancient Anvil:** Aptly renamed centuries ago, this is the oldest known operational forge/blacksmith shop in the world. It has been in the Silvervein family for countless generations and is currently run by Zaulder Silvervein.
* **Echoing Hammer:** Known for specialty armor, this shop is owned and run by Mia Degrain, a retired adventurer and granddaughter of the shop's founder, Revani Degrain.

#### Prominent People
* **Mayor Dzune Gidenven:** A retired Guild-master who is determined to take care of his city by taking care of its people. He is an old dwarf with white hair and grandfather vibes, but remains sharp, shrewd, and always has sweets in his pockets for the city’s children.
* **Head Craftsman Jia Coppervine:** Headmistress of the Craftsmen College, this rock gnome was once one of the best metalworkers on the continent before arthritis and carpal tunnel stole her fine motor control. She ensures everyone has an opportunity to learn and has a kind heart beneath a stern exterior.
* **Guild-master Jhobe Silvervein:** Has been the Guild-Master for nearly five decades. He is a harsh dwarf who heavily favors other dwarves and is currently estranged from his son, Zaulder. He stubbornly believes the Silvervein family is the heart and soul of the city.
* **Abelina Sharpstone:** Owner of the Empty Mine hostel who enjoys singing as she works. Her mother was a famous bard, and Abelina was raised on the road.
* **Durwin Thap:** A human hoarder who owns the Crow’s Nest. He is a paranoid, retired adventurer who nearly died because his past party didn’t have the proper tools for the job.
* **Jed and Ajhed Ortevuni:** Twin dwarven brothers who own Glitter ‘N Gold. They tease each other constantly and fight over designs. 
* **Veccan Thesierra:** A tall female Dragonborn who runs security for the Ortevuni brothers. She doesn't talk much, but legends suggest she once fought a dragon barehanded and won.
* **Zaulder Silvervein:** Owns and runs the Ancient Anvil. He is the estranged son of Jhobe Silvervein, a tired blacksmith widower who has little patience for small talk and refuses to discuss his father.
* **Mia Degrain:** The relatively young owner and lead armorer of the Echoing Hammer, who inherited the business from her grandmother and is a retired adventurer.

#### Nearby Townships and Villages
* **Ironhaven:** A human mining town east of Veladhur, now mostly inhabited by dwarves and gnomes after most human miners left. Strangely enough, it is famous for its honey exports rather than iron, because one of the original founders became a beekeeper.
* **Dhuran:** A halfling town about five miles west of Stonehaven, known for its pottery and whiskey.
* **Venragh:** A hill dwarf and halfling trade-post that turned into a bustling city north of Dhuran.
* **Hillsburrow:** A hill dwarf and gnomish town sitting on a flood plain south of Lapuz Lake. It is known for its wood carvings, irrigation channels, rice cultivation, and rice paper manufacturing.
* **Giggeview:** A gnomish city that sits right on the edge of Lapuz Lake.

### City 3: Emberden

#### General Overview
Emberden was founded by humans but made famous by the many gnomish artisans and inventors that settled there. Sprawling across the Ember Cliffs on the Western coast, the city is the largest port on the continent and a bustling trade center. The successful port attracted magicians, artisans, and scholars of all species, allowing its peaceful (if greedy) nature to turn it into the Third Great City. While it is the youngest of the Three Cities, it is considered one of the Wonders of the world due to its unique integration with the iconic white wood of the Delta.

#### Appearance and Layout
The Ember Cliffs are a hundred feet high and bleached white from sea salt; at sunset, they seem to glow like embers. Half of the city is carved directly into these cliffs, while enchanted, lilac-stained wooden staircases connect the various levels.

The city is structured vertically across the cliffs:
* **The Port:** Sprawls across the water at the base of the cliffs in a web of wooden docks and stone piers.
* **Water Town:** Located at the southern edge of the Port where the water turns brackish. Built upon flimsy piers, this area serves as the slums of the city.
* **The Faces:** The commercial districts and high-middle class residential areas built directly into the side of the whitewashed cliff-face. This area is intricately connected with stone and lilac-wood staircases interspersed like veins.
* **The Tunnels:** Deep excavations stretching into the cliffside behind the Faces.

#### Significant Locations
* **Emberden College:** Attracts academics from across the world and stands as a bitter rival to Æressari University.
* **Doldren's Smithery:** Nestled on the edge of the Tunnels where only a small patch of sky can be seen, this forge is owned by Doldren Brumvar.
* **Sunset Square:** The best marketplace in the city, located on a small, natural ledge within the Faces. It is a cluster of a dozen favored local shops and is one of the oldest parts of the Faces.
* **Mudvin Tavern:** Located on a stone pier between the Port and Water Town, it is the cheapest inn and tavern option for travelers. It is frequented by weary sailors and is known for frequent fistfights. It is run by a large human woman named Margie and a cook named Yavin.
* **Stonegleam Inn:** The most famous (though not most expensive) inn in the city. It is a two-story building carved into the cliff face, decorated with permanent glowing runes that grant it a passive glow.

#### Prominent People
* **Margie:** A large human woman who owns the Mudvin Tavern and can beat even the most stubborn sailors into the ground.
* **Yavin:** The cook for Mudvin Tavern and the best fence in town. He only *sometimes* tries to cheat his customers and may or may not be married to Margie.
* **Doldren Brumvar:** The best blacksmith in the city, though practically no one realizes it. Formerly a famous blacksmith in Veladhur, he moved to Emberden a decade ago following his wife's death to pursue a quiet life.

#### Nearby Townships and Villages
* **Emberridge:** A human-settled satellite city located just a half-mile north of Emberden. It houses several large, wealthy estates belonging to Emberden's elite and is famous for fine food and fancy parties.
* **Esabury:** Located north of Emberden along the coast, this is a small elven town sung from trees growing in sandy soil. It is notoriously isolationist and intensely hates outsiders; everyone who lives there is an elf.
* **Glorre:** A hill dwarf settlement to the east of Emberden and south of Mirevon. They operate massive quarries, supplying the area's stone, and trade frequently with Mirevon.
* **Vlinas:** A medium-sized rock gnome town north of Mirevon dug into rocky dells. It is well-known for goat herding and goat-milk products.
* **Pyre Port:** A medium-sized conglomerate trading post to the north that aggressively attempts to compete with Emberden.

## 4. Leskyir

#### General Overview
Located in the heart of the Merchant’s Pass in the center of the continent, Leskyir is a bustling trade hub. Its strategic location makes it a vital crossroads for vast caravans traversing the world. The surrounding environment is mostly barren desert, dominated by sand and sandstone. Despite the plain landscape, the city is famous for being constantly decked out in incredibly colorful banners and paintings meant to catch the eyes of passing merchants. It is always abuzz with activity, attracting traders, adventurers, and travelers from every corner of the globe.

#### History
Leskyir was originally founded by a collaborative group of merchants who recognized the immense strategic value of its central location. Over centuries of growth, it transitioned into a vital heart of commerce. It has weathered its fair share of conflicts with various factions vying for control of its lucrative trade routes, yet it has always successfully maintained its strict position as a neutral, resilient trade hub.

#### Appearance and Layout
Leskyir is a sprawling city divided into distinct functional districts:
* **Central District:** A chaotic, bustling maze of narrow streets and alleyways where most trading takes place. Small stalls and weathered sandstone shops line the roads, filled with the smells of spices and exotic foods.
* **Residential District:** Located on the city outskirts, this area is uniquely composed of rows of tents made from blankets and wood. Because many residents are transient traders staying temporarily, these tents provide a highly flexible, surprisingly spacious, and comfortable housing solution.

#### Significant Locations
* **Leskyir Palace:** A grand, impressive sandstone structure featuring ornate carvings and decorative features reflecting the city's wealth. It serves as the official seat of government and houses the city's governing council and diplomatic functions.
* **Leskyir Bazaar:** A massive, chaotic market that acts as the beating heart of trade activity. It is split into distinct sections based on the types of goods sold. 
* **The Sandstone Inn:** The largest and most luxurious inn in the city, built entirely of sandstone and decorated with elaborate carvings and murals.
* **The Iron Oasis:** A popular, lively tavern located in the heart of the city. Decorated with a variety of weapons and armor, it is a favored gathering spot for adventurers and mercenaries.
* **The Caravan House:** A heavily guarded, massive warehouse on the outskirts of the city used as the primary storage and distribution center for goods passing through. It is run tightly by a coalition of powerful merchant guilds.
* **Oasis of Delights:** A luxurious brothel catering to adventurers and weary travelers. Its sandstone entrance is carved with intricate patterns and hieroglyphs, opening into a dimly lit, sensual interior filled with plush cushions, vibrant tapestries, and exotic plants.

#### Prominent People
* **The Merchant Princess:** The shrewd and ruthless head of the largest and most powerful merchant guild in Leskyir, controlling a vast majority of the city's commerce.
* **The Desert Guide:** A highly sought-after, seasoned traveler expert at safely navigating caravans through the harsh desert terrain.
* **The Blacksmith:** The most skilled and highly respected artisan in Leskyir, known for his unique designs and expert craftsmanship.
* **The Nomadic Chieftain:** A skilled warrior, diplomat, and leader of one of the largest nomadic tribes in the region, known for his fair and just rule.
* **The Oasis Guardian:** A wise, benevolent, and powerful mage who has dedicated his life to protecting the Oasis of Serenity.
* **The Tavern Keepers:** The charismatic, friendly husband who keeps the Iron Oasis tavern and his gentle wife who is famous for cooking delicious food despite the harsh climate.
* **The Head of the Caravan House:** A stern, calculating, and feared leader of the merchant guilds that control the Caravan House warehouse.
* **The Tribal Elder:** The eldest member of a local nomadic tribe, respected for his deep, ancient knowledge of the desert and its secrets.
* **The Sphinx:** The mysterious, scarlet-robed madame of the Oasis of Delights who is rumored to possess magical powers and ancient knowledge.

#### Nearby Townships and Villages
The desert surrounding Leskyir is populated by nomadic tribes and small settlements, including:
* **The Village of Dust:** A small trading settlement located several miles from Leskyir, widely known for its production of fine textiles and pottery.
* **The Oasis of Serenity:** A lush, beautiful oasis located deep in the desert, heavily visited by travelers for its legendary healing properties and peaceful nature.
* **The Town of Ironwood:** A small mining town on the desert outskirts known for its rich deposits of iron and minerals, making it a popular hub for metalworkers.
* **The Nomadic Camps:** A scattered collection of temporary tribal settlements throughout the desert, each maintaining unique cultural traditions.
