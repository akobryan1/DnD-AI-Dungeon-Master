# Blood Hunter (2020 Edition)
*Class Design by Matthew Mercer*

## Class Overview

Often feared or misunderstood, and driven by an unending drive to destroy the wicked, blood hunters are clever, arcane warriors who have bound their essence to the dark creatures they hunt to better stalk and survive their prey. Armed with the rites of forbidden blood magic and a willingness to sacrifice their own vitality and humanity for the cause, they protect the realms from the shadows, ever vigilant to avoid becoming the same monsters they choose to hunt.

Far from the judging eyes of society, blood hunters have mastered the secretive techniques of hemocraft, finding blood magic's esoteric nature effective against evils that resist divine rebuke or arcane bindings. Through careful study and practice, blood hunters hone the rites of hemocraft into unique combat techniques, forfeiting a portion of their own health to call blood curses down upon their enemies or summon the elements to aid their strikes. Willing to suffer whatever it takes to achieve victory, these adept warriors have forged themselves into a potent force dedicated to protecting the innocent.

## Class Features

**Hit Points**
* **Hit Dice:** 1d10 per Blood Hunter level
* **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your Constitution modifier
* **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per Blood Hunter level after 1st

**Proficiencies**
* **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
* **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
* **Tools:** Alchemist's supplies
* **Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Intelligence
* **Skills:** Choose three from Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, History, Insight, Investigation, Religion, and Survival.

*(Note: While Intelligence is the default Hemocraft modifier in the 2020 version, some DMs allow Wisdom to be substituted. This document uses Intelligence as the standard).*

### Level 1: Hunter's Bane
You have survived the Hunter's Bane—a dangerous, long-guarded ritual that alters your life's blood, forever binding you to the darkness and honing your senses against it. You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track fey, fiends, or undead, as well as on Intelligence checks to recall information about such creatures.
Upon reaching 11th level, you can also roll a hemocraft die and add the number rolled to the check.

### Level 1: Blood Maledict
You gain the ability to channel, and sometimes sacrifice, a part of your vital essence to curse and manipulate creatures through hemocraft magic. You know one Blood Curse of your choice. You learn additional curses as you gain levels (see the table).
When you use a Blood Curse, you can choose to **Amplify** the curse by taking necrotic damage equal to one roll of your hemocraft die. This damage can't be reduced in any way. An amplified curse gains an additional effect, noted in the curse's description. Creatures that do not have blood are immune to blood curses, unless you have amplified the curse.
You can use this feature once. You regain expended uses when you finish a short or long rest.

### Level 2: Fighting Style
You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following options:
* **Archery:** You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with ranged weapons.
* **Dueling:** When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.
* **Great Weapon Fighting:** When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.
* **Two-Weapon Fighting:** When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

### Level 2: Crimson Rite
You learn to invoke a rite of hemocraft within your weapon at the cost of your own vitality. Choose one rite from the primal rites list below. You learn an additional primal rite at 7th level, and an esoteric rite at 14th level.
As a bonus action, you can activate a crimson rite on a single weapon with the elemental energy of a known rite. Until the rite fades, attacks you make with this weapon deal extra damage equal to your hemocraft die of the chosen rite's type.
When you activate a rite, you take necrotic damage equal to one roll of your hemocraft die. This damage can't be reduced in any way, and drops your hit point maximum by an equal amount until the rite ends. The rite fades if the weapon leaves your grip.

**Primal Rites:**
* **Rite of the Flame:** Fire damage.
* **Rite of the Frozen:** Cold damage.
* **Rite of the Storm:** Lightning damage.

**Esoteric Rites (Level 14):**
* **Rite of the Dead:** Necrotic damage.
* **Rite of the Oracle:** Psychic damage.
* **Rite of the Roar:** Thunder damage.

### Level 3: Blood Hunter Order
You commit to an order of blood hunters: the Order of the Ghostslayer, Order of the Lycan, Order of the Mutant, or Order of the Profane Soul. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and again at 7th, 11th, 15th, and 18th level.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

### Level 5: Extra Attack
You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Level 6: Brand of Castigation
Whenever you damage a creature with your Crimson Rite feature, you can choose to sear an arcane brand of hemocraft magic into it (requires no action). You always know the direction to the branded creature, and each time the branded creature deals damage to you or a creature you can see within 5 feet of you, the creature takes psychic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1 damage).
Your brand lasts until you dismiss it, or you apply a brand to another creature.

### Level 9: Grim Psychometry
You have a supernatural talent for discerning the history surrounding mysterious objects or places touched by evil. When making an Intelligence (History) check to recall info about a darker past, you have advantage. You can also meditate on an object for 10 minutes to discern lingering evil or vague visions of its past.

### Level 10: Dark Augmentation
Your body physically adapts to the toxicity of your hemocraft. Your speed increases by 5 feet, and whenever you make a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution saving throw, you gain a bonus to the saving throw equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1).

### Level 13: Brand of Tethering
The psychic damage from your Brand of Castigation increases to twice your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 2 damage). In addition, a branded creature can't take the Dash action, and if it attempts to teleport or leave its current plane, it takes 4d6 psychic damage and must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the attempt fails.

### Level 14: Hardened Soul
You gain advantage on saving throws against being charmed and frightened.

### Level 20: Sanguine Mastery
When you are below half your hit point maximum, all your hemocraft die rolls are treated as rolling the maximum value. Also, when you roll a critical hit with a weapon imbued with your Crimson Rite, you regain one expended use of your Blood Maledict feature.

## Blood Curses

Here are the primary blood curses available to blood hunters:

* **Blood Curse of the Anxious:** As a bonus action, you can make a creature more susceptible to coercion. Until the end of your next turn, Intimidation checks against them have advantage. *Amplify:* The target also has disadvantage on its next Wisdom saving throw.
* **Blood Curse of Binding:** As a bonus action, you can attempt to bind an enemy no more than one size larger than you within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of your next turn. *Amplify:* This curse lasts for 1 minute, with saves at the end of each of their turns.
* **Blood Curse of Bloated Agony:** As a bonus action, you curse a creature to swell until the end of your next turn. It has disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks, and takes 1d8 necrotic damage if it makes more than one melee or ranged attack on its turn. *Amplify:* Lasts for 1 minute (saves at end of turns).
* **Blood Curse of Exposure:** When a creature you can see within 30 feet takes damage from an attack or spell, you can use your reaction to temporarily weaken its resilience. The target loses resistance to the damage types of the triggering attack. *Amplify:* The target loses invulnerability to the damage types, instead having resistance.
* **Blood Curse of the Eyeless:** When a creature makes an attack roll, you can use your reaction to roll one hemocraft die and subtract the number rolled from the creature's attack roll. *Amplify:* You apply this curse to all of the creature's attack rolls until the end of the turn.
* **Blood Curse of the Fallen Puppet:** When a creature falls unconscious or dies within 30 feet, you can use your reaction to make them immediately attack one target of your choice within their weapon's reach. *Amplify:* You also grant a bonus to the attack roll and damage roll equal to your Intelligence modifier.
* **Blood Curse of the Marked:** As a bonus action, you mark a creature within 30 feet. Until the end of your turn, your Crimson Rite deals double damage to the target. *Amplify:* You ensure your next attack roll against the target has advantage.
* **Blood Curse of the Muddled Mind:** As a bonus action, you curse a creature concentrating on a spell. The next time it makes a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration, it has disadvantage. *Amplify:* The target also takes 3d6 psychic damage if it fails the concentration check.

## Blood Hunter Orders (Subclasses)

### Order of the Ghostslayer
The Order of the Ghostslayer is the oldest of the orders, having rediscovered the secrets of hemocraft and adapting them to battle the scourge of undeath.

* **Rite of the Dawn (Level 3):** You learn the Rite of the Dawn (Radiant damage). Your weapon sheds bright light out to 20 feet. You have resistance to necrotic damage. When you hit an undead creature with this rite active, you roll an additional hemocraft die for damage. You do not suffer maximum hit point reduction when activating this rite against undead.
* **Curse Specialist (Level 3):** You learn one additional Blood Curse. Also, your Blood Curses can target creatures without blood (like undead) without needing to be amplified.
* **Ethereal Step (Level 7):** At the start of your turn, you can magically step into the veil between planes. You can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain, and you gain a hover speed equal to your walking speed. You take 1d10 force damage if you end your turn inside an object. Lasts for a number of rounds equal to your INT modifier.
* **Brand of Sundering (Level 11):** Your Brand of Castigation now exposes a fragment of the target's essence. The branded creature can't move through creatures or objects, and any illusion disguising them is suppressed. In addition, you have advantage on attack rolls against the branded creature.
* **Blood Curse of the Exorcist (Level 15):** You learn this unique curse. As a bonus action, you can choose a creature within 30 feet that is charmed, frightened, or possessed, ending the condition. *Amplify:* The creature that caused the condition takes 3d6 psychic damage and must make a Wisdom save or be stunned until the end of your next turn.
* **Rite Revival (Level 18):** When you drop to 0 hit points, you can choose to let your active Crimson Rite fade, dropping the weapon to immediately end your turn and drop to 1 hit point instead.

### Order of the Lycan
Of the many terrible curses that plague the realm, few are as ancient or as feared as Lycanthropy. The Order of the Lycan is a proud order of blood hunters who undergo "The Taming", inflicting themselves with a controlled strain of the disease.

* **Heightened Senses (Level 3):** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
* **Hybrid Transformation (Level 3):** You can use a bonus action to transform into a Hybrid Lycan for up to 1 hour. You can use this feature twice per short/long rest. Benefits:
    * **Feral Might:** Advantage on Strength checks and saves, and +1 to melee damage rolls (scales to +2 at 11th, +3 at 18th).
    * **Resilient Hide:** Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks not made with silvered weapons. +1 to AC if not in heavy armor.
    * **Predatory Strikes:** Your unarmed strikes are treated as a single weapon and can have Crimson Rites applied to them. They use DEX or STR, and deal 1d6 damage (scales to 1d8 at 11th).
    * **Bloodlust:** If you begin your turn with less than half your maximum hit points, you must make a Wisdom save (DC 8 or DC equal to damage taken). On a failure, you move directly toward the nearest creature and use the Attack action against it.
* **Stalker's Prowess (Level 7):** Your speed increases by 10 feet. Your jump distance is doubled. Your unarmed strikes in hybrid form are considered magical.
* **Advanced Transformation (Level 11):** You can use your Hybrid Transformation three times per short rest. You passively regenerate 1 + your CON modifier hit points at the start of your turn if you have at least 1 HP but less than half your max HP.
* **Brand of the Voracious (Level 15):** Your Brand of Castigation binds you to your prey. While in hybrid form, you have advantage on attacks against your branded target, and the psychic damage they take from the brand ignores resistances.
* **Hybrid Terror Form (Level 18):** You can now use your Blood Curse feature and amplify it without taking damage while in your hybrid form. Also, you can unleash a terrifying howl (Wisdom save to enemies or become frightened).

### Order of the Mutant
The process of the Hunter's Bane is a painful, scarring, and sometimes fatal experience. Those of the Order of the Mutant study the science of hemocraft and alchemy to alter their own biology further, trading temporary physical drawbacks for incredible bursts of power.

* **Mutagencraft (Level 3):** You can craft Mutagens during a short or long rest. You learn 4 formulas at 3rd level. You can carry one mutagen at a time (more at higher levels). Consuming a mutagen is a bonus action. The effects (and the associated side effect) last until you finish a short or long rest, or until you flush them out of your system with an action.
    * *Sample Formulas:* Celerity (+DEX score, disadvantage WIS saves), Potency (+STR score, disadvantage DEX saves), Sagacity (+INT score, disadvantage CHA saves), Reconstruction (Regen HP, minus 10ft speed), Cruelty (Extra attack bonus action, disadvantage on all saves).
* **Strange Metabolism (Level 7):** As a bonus action, you can ignore the negative side effects of a single mutagen affecting you for 1 minute.
* **Brand of Axiom (Level 11):** Your Brand of Castigation forces a creature into its true form. If the branded creature is polymorphed or shapechanged, it must succeed on a Wisdom save or revert. It cannot shapechange again while branded. Illusions cast by the creature are dispelled.
* **Blood Curse of Corrosion (Level 15):** You learn this unique curse. As a bonus action, you curse a creature within 30 feet to become poisoned. *Amplify:* The target also takes 4d6 necrotic damage, and takes this damage again every turn it fails the save to end the poisoned condition.
* **Exalted Mutation (Level 18):** You can now craft up to three mutagens at a time. Whenever you consume a mutagen, you can choose to flush out one currently active mutagen from your system as part of the same bonus action.

### Order of the Profane Soul
Those who join the Order of the Profane Soul have stepped further into the shadows, making pacts with lesser evils to better combat the greater. They strike bargains with fiends, fey, or old gods, gaining arcane casting capabilities.

* **Pact Magic (Level 3):** You gain the ability to cast spells. You use the Warlock spell list.
    * **Cantrips:** You learn two Warlock cantrips.
    * **Spell Slots:** You have a limited number of spell slots, all of which are at a specific level (starting at 1st level, capping at 4th level at Blood Hunter level 19). They recharge on a short or long rest.
    * **Spellcasting Ability:** Intelligence is your spellcasting ability.
* **Patron (Level 3):** You choose a patron (The Archfey, The Celestial, The Fathomless, The Fiend, The Genie, The Great Old One, The Hexblade, or The Undying). This choice grants specific buffs to your Crimson Rite.
    * *Example (The Fiend):* While using the Rite of the Flame, if you roll a 1 on your hemocraft die for damage, you can reroll it once.
* **Mystic Frenzy (Level 7):** When you use your action to cast a cantrip, you can immediately make one weapon attack as a bonus action.
* **Revealed Arcana (Level 7):** Your patron grants you access to a specific 1st-level spell based on your patron choice, which you can cast once per long rest without expending a spell slot.
* **Brand of the Scar (Level 11):** Your Brand of Castigation leaves a supernatural vulnerability. Whenever you deal damage with a spell or cantrip to a branded creature, you roll an additional hemocraft die of damage. Also, they have disadvantage on saving throws against your warlock spells.
* **Unsealed Arcana (Level 15):** Your patron grants you access to a specific 4th-level spell based on your patron choice, castable once per long rest without a spell slot.
* **Blood Curse of the Souleater (Level 18):** You learn this unique curse. When a creature that isn't a construct or undead drops to 0 HP within 30 feet of you, you can use your reaction to consume their soul. You regain HP equal to your Blood Hunter level + INT modifier. *Amplify:* In addition to the healing, you also regain an expended warlock spell slot.
