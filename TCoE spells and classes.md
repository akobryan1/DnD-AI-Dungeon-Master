# Spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

This document contains the full descriptions of all new spells introduced in *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* that are present in the provided source text. Some spells (e.g., *booming blade*, *sword burst*, *tasha's caustic brew*, *tasha's mind whip*, *blade of disaster*, *dream of the blue veil*, *summon undead*) are listed in class spell lists but their descriptions are missing from the OCR. Only spells with complete mechanics are included below.

## Green-Flame Blade
*Evocation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Self (5-foot radius)  
**Components:** S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  

You brandish the weapon used in the spell's casting and make a melee attack with it against one creature within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target suffers the weapon attack's normal effects, and you can cause green fire to leap from the target to a different creature of your choice that you can see within 5 feet of it. The second creature takes fire damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier.

This spell's damage increases when you reach certain levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra 1d8 fire damage to the target on a hit, and the fire damage to the second creature increases to 1d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier. Both damage rolls increase by 1d8 at 11th level (2d8 and 2d8) and 17th level (3d8 and 3d8).

## Intellect Fortress
*3rd-level abjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour  

For the duration, you or one willing creature you can see within range has resistance to psychic damage, as well as advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.

## Lightning Lure
*Evocation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Self (15-foot radius)  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  

You create a lash of lightning energy that strikes at one creature of your choice that you can see within 15 feet of you. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 10 feet in a straight line toward you and then take 1d8 lightning damage if it is within 5 feet of you.

This spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

## Mind Sliver
*Enchantment cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** 1 round  

You drive a disorienting spike of psychic energy into the mind of one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw or take 1d6 psychic damage and subtract 1d4 from the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn.

This spell's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach certain levels: 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).

## Spirit Shroud
*3rd-level necromancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute  

You call forth spirits of the dead, which fit around you for the spell's duration. The spirits are intangible and invulnerable.

Until the spell ends, any attack you make deals 1d8 extra damage when you hit a creature within 10 feet of you. This damage is radiant, necrotic, or cold (your choice when you cast the spell). Any creature that takes this damage can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

In addition, any creature of your choice that you can see that starts its turn within 10 feet of you has its speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for every two slot levels above 3rd.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the spell's level appears in the stat block.

## Tasha's Otherworldly Guise
*6th-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (an object engraved with a symbol of the Outer Planes, worth at least 500 gp)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute  

Uttering an incantation, you draw on the magic of the Lower Planes or Upper Planes (your choice) to transform yourself. You gain the following benefits until the spell ends:

- You are immune to fire and poison damage (Lower Planes) or radiant and necrotic damage (Upper Planes).
- You are immune to the poisoned condition (Lower Planes) or the charmed condition (Upper Planes).
- Spectral wings appear on your back, giving you a flying speed of 40 feet.
- You have a +2 bonus to AC.
- All your weapon attacks are magical, and when you make a weapon attack, you can use your spellcasting ability modifier, instead of Strength or Dexterity, for the attack and damage rolls.
- You can attack twice, instead of once, when you take the Attack action on your turn. You ignore this benefit if you already have a feature, like Extra Attack, that lets you attack more than once when you take the Attack action on your turn.

## Artificer

Masters of invention, artificers use ingenuity and magic to unlock extraordinary capabilities in objects.

### Class Features

**Hit Points**
- **Hit Dice:** 1d8 per artificer level
- **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + Constitution modifier per level after 1st

**Proficiencies**
- **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons
- **Tools:** Thieves' tools, tinker's tools, one type of artisan's tools of your choice
- **Saving Throws:** Constitution, Intelligence
- **Skills:** Choose two from Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Sleight of Hand

**Multiclassing**
- **Ability Score Minimum:** Intelligence 13
- **Proficiencies Gained:** Light armor, medium armor, shields, thieves' tools, tinker's tools
- **Spell Slots:** Add half your levels (rounded up) in artificer to other classes.

### Class Features

**Magical Tinkering (1st)**
You can touch a Tiny nonmagical object and give it one of the following magical properties: shed light (5 ft. bright +5 dim), emit a recorded message (6 seconds) when tapped, emit an odor or nonverbal sound (up to 10 ft.), or display a static visual effect (picture, up to 25 words). You can affect a number of objects equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

**Spellcasting (1st)**
You cast artificer spells using your tools as a focus. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability. You prepare spells equal to Intelligence modifier + half your artificer level (rounded down). You can cast spells as rituals if they have the ritual tag.

**Infuse Item (2nd)**
You learn infusions (see Artificer Infusions section). After a long rest, you can touch a nonmagical object and imbue it with an infusion, turning it into a magic item. You can infuse a number of objects equal to your Infused Items column.

**The Right Tool for the Job (3rd)**
With thieves' tools or artisan's tools in hand, you can magically create one set of artisan's tools in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you after 1 hour of work.

**Ability Score Improvement (4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, 19th)**
Increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Tool Expertise (6th)**
Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check using a tool you're proficient with.

**Flash of Genius (7th)**
When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet makes an ability check or saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier. Uses equal to Intelligence modifier (min 1), regained on long rest.

**Magic Item Adept (10th)**
You can attune to up to four magic items. Crafting common or uncommon magic items takes a quarter of the normal time and half the gold.

**Spell-Storing Item (11th)**

## Fighter

Optional class features, new fighting styles, maneuvers, and martial archetypes.

### Optional Class Features

**Fighting Style Options (1st)**

- **Interception:** When a creature you can see hits a target (other than you) within 5 ft., you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by 1d10 + proficiency bonus (min 0). Must be wielding a shield or a simple/martial weapon.
- **Superior Technique:** You learn one Battle Master maneuver, and you gain one superiority die (d6). The save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier.
- **Thrown Weapon Fighting:** You can draw a thrown weapon as part of the attack you make with it. When you hit with a ranged attack using a thrown weapon, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll.
- **Unarmed Fighting:** Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 + Strength modifier on a hit (1d8 if you aren't wielding any weapons or a shield). At the start of each of your turns, you can deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage to one creature grappled by you.

**Martial Versatility (4th)**
When you reach a level that grants Ability Score Improvement, you can replace a fighting style you know with another fighting style available to fighters, or replace a Battle Master maneuver you know with a different maneuver.

### Maneuver Options

**Ambush**
When you make a Dexterity (Stealth) check or initiative roll, you can expend a superiority die and add it to the roll (provided you aren't incapacitated).

**Bait and Switch**
When you're within 5 ft. of a creature on your turn, you can expend a superiority die and switch places with that creature (requires at least 5 ft. of movement, willing creature). Roll the die; you or the other creature (your choice) gains a bonus to AC equal to the roll until the start of your next turn.

**Brace**
When a creature you can see moves into your melee weapon's reach, you can use your reaction to expend a superiority die and make one attack against that creature. If it hits, add the die to the damage.

**Commanding Presence**
When you make a Charisma (Intimidation), Charisma (Performance), or Charisma (Persuasion) check, you can expend a superiority die and add it to the check.

**Grappling Strike**
Immediately after you hit a creature with a melee attack on your turn, you can expend a superiority die and then try to grapple the target as a bonus action. Add the superiority die to your Strength (Athletics) check.

**Quick Toss**
As a bonus action, you can expend a superiority die and make a ranged attack with a thrown weapon (draw as part of the attack). If hit, add the die to damage.

**Tactical Assessment**
When you make an Intelligence (Investigation), Intelligence (History), or Wisdom (Insight) check, you can expend a superiority die and add it to the check.

Monks who manifest their astral self.

- Use Wisdom modifier for Strength checks and saves.

- **Astral Sight:** See normally in darkness (magical and nonmagical) up to 120 ft.
- **Wisdom of the Spirit:** Advantage on Insight and Intimidation checks.
- **Word of the Spirit:** Speak to a creature within 60 ft. so only it can hear you, or amplify voice to 600 ft.

**Body of the Astral Self (11th)**
When both arms and visage are summoned, you can cause the body to appear (no action). Benefits:
- **Deflect Energy:** When you take acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, or thunder damage, you can use reaction to reduce damage by 1d10 + Wisdom modifier.
- **Empowered Arms:** Once per turn when you hit with Arms, deal extra damage equal to your Martial Arts die.

**Awakened Astral Self (17th)**
As a bonus action, spend 5 ki points to summon arms, visage, and body and awaken them for 10 minutes. Benefits:
- **Armor of the Spirit:** +2 bonus to AC.
- **Astral Barrage:** When you use Extra Attack, you can instead attack three times if all attacks are made with your astral arms.

- **Psychic Whispers:** As an action, choose up to proficiency bonus creatures you can see, roll one Psionic Energy die. For hours equal to the roll, they can speak telepathically with you within 1 mile. The first time you use this after a long rest, you don't expend the die.

**Psychic Blades (3rd)**
When you take the Attack action, you can manifest a psychic blade from your free hand. It is a simple melee weapon with finesse and thrown (normal range 60 ft., no long range). On a hit, it deals 1d6 + ability modifier psychic damage. The blade vanishes after hit or miss. After you attack with the blade, you can make a second attack with a different hand (the blade disappears after that attack). The psychic blade counts as a monk weapon.

**Soul Blades (9th)**
- **Homing Strikes:** When you miss with a Psychic Blades attack, you can roll one Psionic Energy die and add it to the attack roll. If it hits, you expend the die.
- **Psychic Teleportation:** As a bonus action, manifest a blade, expend one die and roll it, throw the blade up to 10 × the number rolled feet to an unoccupied space, then teleport there.

**Psychic Veil (13th)**
As an action, you become invisible (along with equipment) for 1 hour, until you deal damage or force a saving throw. Once used, can't again until long rest, unless you expend a Psionic Energy die.

**Rend Mind (17th)**
When you use Psychic Blades to deal Sneak Attack damage, you can force the target to make a Wisdom save (DC 8 + proficiency + Dexterity modifier). On fail, the target is stunned for 1 minute (save repeats at end of each turn). Once used, can't again until long rest, unless you expend three Psionic Energy dice.

**Bond of the Talisman (12th level, Pact of the Talisman)**

When the wearer of your talisman is hit by an attacker you can see within 30 ft., you can use your reaction to deal psychic damage to the attacker equal to your proficiency bonus and push it up to 10 ft. away from the wearer.

**Cantrip Formulas (3rd)**
After a long rest and consulting your spellbook, you can replace one wizard cantrip you know with another from the wizard spell list.

### Bladesinging

Wizards who incorporate swordplay and dance.

**Training in War and Song (2nd)**
You gain proficiency with light armor and one one-handed melee weapon of your choice. You also gain proficiency in Performance if you don't already have it.

**Bladesong (2nd)**
As a bonus action, you can start a Bladesong for 1 minute (not wearing medium/heavy armor or shield). It ends early if you are incapacitated, don medium/heavy armor or shield, or use two hands to make a weapon attack. Benefits:
- Bonus to AC equal to your Intelligence modifier (min +1)
- Walking speed increases by 10 ft.
- Advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks
- Bonus to Constitution saves to maintain concentration equal to Intelligence modifier

Use a number of times equal to proficiency bonus, regain on long rest.

**Extra Attack (6th)**
You can attack twice when you take the Attack action. Moreover, you can cast one of your cantrips in place of one of those attacks.

**Song of Defense (10th)**
When you take damage while Bladesong is active, you can use your reaction to expend a spell slot and reduce the damage by an amount equal to five times the spell slot's level.

**Song of Victory (14th)**
While your Bladesong is active, you add your Intelligence modifier (min +1) to the damage of your melee weapon attacks.

### Order of Scribes

Wizards who magically awaken their spellbook.

**Wizardly Quill (2nd)**
As a bonus action, you can create a Tiny quill. It doesn't require ink; it produces ink in a color of your choice. Copying a spell into your spellbook takes 2 minutes per spell level with the quill. You can erase anything you write with it as a bonus action.

**Awakened Spellbook (2nd)**
While holding your spellbook, you gain:
- The book as a spellcasting focus.
- When you cast a wizard spell with a spell slot, you can temporarily replace its damage type with a type that appears in another spell in your spellbook of the same level.
- When you cast a wizard spell as a ritual, you can use the normal casting time instead of adding 10 minutes (once per long rest).

If necessary, you can replace the book over a short rest using your quill.

**Manifest Mind (6th)**
As a bonus action while the book is on your person, you can cause its mind to manifest as a Tiny spectral object within 60 ft. It sheds dim light in 10 ft., has darkvision 60 ft., and can telepathically share what it sees/hears. When you cast a wizard spell on your turn, you can cast it as if you were in the mind's space (use its senses). You can do this a number of times per day equal to your proficiency bonus. As a bonus action, move the mind up to 30 ft. It stops if more than 300 ft. away, dispelled, or dismissed. Once conjured, you can't again until long rest, unless you expend a spell slot.

**Master Scrivener (10th)**
After a long rest, you can create one magic scroll from your spellbook (1st or 2nd level, 1 action casting time). The spell on the scroll is enhanced, counting as one level higher. The scroll is unintelligible to others and vanishes when cast or at the end of your next long rest. Crafting spell scrolls takes half the gold and time with your quill.

**One with the Word (14th)**
While the book is on your person, you have advantage on all Intelligence (Arcana) checks. If you take damage while your spellbook's mind is manifested, you can use your reaction to dismiss the mind and prevent all that damage. Then roll 3d6; the spellbook temporarily loses spells of your choice with a combined level equal to that roll or higher. For 1d6 long rests, you can't cast those lost spells. After the required rests, the spells reappear. Once used, can't again until long rest.
